Court Rules

COURT RULES

Please help keep our courts safe, clean, and enjoyable for everyone.

1

Booking & Arrival

Reservations only — book through our online system. Arrive 10 minutes before your slot.

2

Proper Footwear

Non-marking athletic shoes required. No slippers, sandals, or barefoot play.

3

Court Use

Use the court only during your booked hours. Vacate promptly when your session ends.

4

Food, Drinks & Smoking

No food, chewing gum, smoking, or vaping on the courts. Water and sports drinks are fine. No breakable bottles.

5

Equipment

Use approved paddles and balls only.

6

Sportsmanship

Be respectful, call your own lines fairly, and keep the game friendly.

7

Safety First

Play at your own risk. Warm up before playing and report any injuries or hazards immediately.

8

Clean Facility

Dispose of trash properly. Do not climb, hang, or lean on nets, fences, or barriers.

9

Children

Children must be supervised by an adult at all times.

10

Payment & Cancellation

Bookings are confirmed once payment (GCash) is verified. Cancel or reschedule at least 1 day in advance.

Management may refuse entry or suspend booking privileges for rule violations.
